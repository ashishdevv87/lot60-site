Lot60 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Lot60 is a Chrome extension that helps car dealership staff turn a vehicle on their dealership's website into a Facebook Marketplace vehicle listing. This policy explains what information Lot60 handles and where it goes.

The short version

Lot60 has no servers and does not send any information to the developer of Lot60. Everything Lot60 saves stays in the Chrome browser on your computer. The only information Lot60 sends to an outside service is a vehicle's VIN, to the U.S. government's free VIN decoder, as explained below.

Information Lot60 saves on your computer

  • Dealership setup: dealership name, used-inventory web address, dealer code, documentation fee, phone number and the perks and services you choose to include in ads.
  • Salesperson setups: each salesperson's name, phone number, ad instructions and example ad.
  • Vehicles you import: details shown on your dealership's website, such as title, price, kilometres, VIN, stock number, features, vehicle history highlights and photo addresses.

This information is stored with Chrome's extension storage on your computer. It is not synced to your Google account and is not shared with other computers. Removing Lot60 from Chrome deletes it.

Websites Lot60 reads and fills in

  • Your dealership's website: when you click to load inventory or import a vehicle, Lot60 reads the vehicle pages in your browser. Chrome asks for your permission before Lot60 can access a website.
  • Facebook: when you click Prepare in Facebook or Fill open Facebook form, Lot60 fills in the Marketplace vehicle form in your own Facebook session and attaches the vehicle photos you selected. Lot60 never clicks Next, Save draft or Publish; you review and publish the listing yourself. What you publish on Facebook is covered by Facebook's own terms and privacy policy.
  • VIN decoder: when you prepare a listing, Lot60 sends that vehicle's VIN (vehicle identification number) to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's public vPIC service (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov) to confirm factory details such as drivetrain, seating rows and safety equipment. Only the VIN is sent: no names, phone numbers, prices or other information. A VIN identifies a vehicle, not a person. NHTSA's handling of requests is covered by its own privacy policy.
  • Vehicle photos: Lot60 downloads the photos you selected from your dealership's photo host so it can attach them to the Facebook form.

Clipboard

When you click Copy reviewed description, Lot60 copies that listing description to your clipboard. It does not read your clipboard.

Settings files

Export settings saves a file to your computer containing your dealership and salesperson setups. Lot60 does not upload it anywhere. Keep it private if it contains phone numbers.

What Lot60 does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies of its own.
  • No selling or sharing of information.
  • No collection of passwords, payment details or browsing history.
